Hi! I'm **Tei Brito** and this is **SaruChan Videogames**. Welcome to the devlog: this is where I'll share how the studio's games are made, with all their sketches, ridiculous bugs and small victories.

## The studio

SaruChan Videogames is a one-person independent studio. Design, code, art and testing all come from the same pair of hands, and everything is built in **Godot**.

The name comes from *saru* (猿), "monkey" in Japanese, and it's also my nickname for my son. That's where our mascot comes from: Saru, a monkey with glasses who watches everything, tests everything and now and then breaks something. That's how you learn.

Before the current projects came a handful of small games that you can still play for free on [itch.io](https://saruchanvideogames.itch.io). They were my school. Now it's time to go for something bigger.

## Hikari

**Hikari** is a 2D action platformer in the metroidvania style, and it's the studio's flagship project.

You play as Hikari, a student who is summoned to another world, a world that is slowly dying, devoured by the Darkness. Her mission, according to those who brought her there, is to defeat the Demon King. But Hikari refuses to be just another pawn: she wants to know why she was chosen, what happened to the ones who came before, and who decided that destruction was the only way out.

Her weapons are born from light and change shape mid-combat: **sword**, **shield**, **shot** and **hammer**. Each form is for fighting and also for opening new paths, because in a good metroidvania exploring matters as much as fighting.

In this world, exploring isn't about ticking icons off a map. It's about understanding what went wrong and who was to blame.

## What's next

The first goal is a **playable demo of about ten minutes**: a complete area, a boss and a new ability that opens the way to what was locked before. Right now I'm polishing the movement (jumps, falls and transitions between rooms), because if moving doesn't feel good, nothing else matters.

I'll keep sharing progress here and on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/saruchanvideogames.bsky.social) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/saruchan_videogames).
